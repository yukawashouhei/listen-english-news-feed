AB News

Daily news stories rewritten at five levels of English, with translations, narration and a word book.

Support

Questions or problems? Email yukawa.shouhei@gmail.com. Please include your iOS version and the language you use in the app.

Subscriptions

AB News Pass is billed through your Apple Account and can be managed or cancelled in iOS Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. The 7-day free trial converts to a paid subscription unless cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends.
