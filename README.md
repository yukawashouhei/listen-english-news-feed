# AB News feed

Static content for the AB News app (daily graded English news). Generated by the
private `abnews` repository's edition builder; nothing here is edited by hand.

Layout: `feed/v1/index.json`, `feed/v1/stories/<id>.json`, `feed/v1/text/<id>/<lang>.json`,
`feed/v1/voice/<id>/<tier>-<n>.m4a`.

Word lists used for vocabulary bands: NGSL, NAWL and TSL by Browne, Culligan & Phillips
(newgeneralservicelist.com), CC BY-SA 4.0.
