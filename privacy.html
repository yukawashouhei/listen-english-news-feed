Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

Listen English News ("the app") is published by Shohei Yukawa. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

What the app stores on your device

Your chosen topics, exam, level, language, playback speed, saved words and downloaded stories and audio are stored only on your device (and in your iCloud device backup if enabled). The app has no accounts.

What leaves your device

  • Content downloads. The app fetches stories, translations and audio from our static content server over HTTPS. Standard server logs (IP address, time, file requested) may be kept briefly by the hosting provider (GitHub Pages).
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts, never your name or email. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • On-device translation. When a translation is not shipped with a story, iOS translates it on your device using Apple's Translation framework. Text is not sent to us.

What we do not do

No advertising SDKs, no analytics, no tracking across apps, no sale of data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
