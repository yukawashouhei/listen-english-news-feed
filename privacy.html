プライバシーポリシー

2026 年 11 月 22 日 改定(English below)

英語日記ペンギン(旧名 Listen English News。iPhone・iPad・Android のアプリ。以下「本アプリ」)の運営者(以下「運営者」)が、利用者の情報をどう扱うかを定めます。ウェブ版(eigonikki.pages.dev)はウェブ版のプライバシーポリシーをご覧ください。

1. 運営者が持たない情報

  • アカウント登録はありません。名前・メールアドレス・電話番号・住所は受け取りません。
  • 日記の文・英文・音声は、運営者のサーバーに保存しません。
  • 広告は出しません。アプリやウェブサイトをまたいだ追跡(トラッキング)はしません。広告 ID は使いません。情報を売ることはありません。

2. 端末の中に保存するもの

日記(日本語の文・英文・音声・解説)、保存した単語カード、設定、ダウンロードした記事は、お使いの端末の中にだけ保存します(端末のバックアップを有効にしている場合は、iPhone は iCloud、Android は Google のバックアップにも含まれます)。日記は設定からファイルに書き出し・読み込みができます。「カレンダーに追加」をオンにした場合だけ、日記をカレンダーの予定として端末のカレンダーに書き込みます。カレンダーの内容を外部に送ることはありません。

3. 外部に送る情報(電気通信事業法の外部送信についての公表を含みます)

次の表のとおり、機能を使うときに必要な情報だけを送ります。送るときは常に暗号化通信(HTTPS)です。

4. 情報を消すには

  • 端末の中の日記・カード・設定は、アプリを削除すると消えます。
  • 中継サーバーの回数の記録は 2 日で自動的に消えます。
  • RevenueCat に残る購入の記録の削除は、下記のお問い合わせ先にご連絡ください。購入時の領収書にある注文 ID(Google Play は注文番号)を書き添えてください。

5. 第三者への提供

上の表の送信先のほかに、利用者の情報を第三者に提供することはありません(法令に基づく場合を除きます)。

6. 子どもの利用

本アプリは 13 歳未満の子どもを対象としていません。

7. 改定

送信先や送る情報が変わるときは、このページを改定してから変更します。

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

English Diary Penguin (formerly Listen English News; the iPhone, iPad and Android app, "the app") is run by an individual developer ("we"). The Japanese text above is the authoritative version. The web version has its own policy.

What we do not hold

There is no sign-up. We never receive your name, email, phone number or address. Your diary text, English text and audio are not stored on our servers. No ads, no tracking across apps or websites, no advertising identifier, no sale of data.

On your device

Diary entries (Japanese, English, audio, notes), saved word cards, settings and downloaded stories stay on your device (and in your device backup, iCloud on iPhone or Google on Android, if enabled). If you turn on "Add to Calendar", each diary is written to your device calendar; nothing from the calendar is sent anywhere.

What leaves your device

  • Our relay server (Cloudflare Workers). When you tidy, translate, correct or voice a diary, the text, the chosen level and voice, a random app user ID (the RevenueCat anonymous ID) and, as part of any connection, your IP address reach our relay, which passes the text to the AI services below. The relay does not store the text or the audio. It keeps only daily request counts (deleted after 2 days), a 10-minute cache of whether the pass is active, and a flag for whether the free natural voice has been used (kept).
  • Anthropic, PBC (Claude) receives the diary text (no name or user ID) to tidy, translate, correct and explain it. Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days; inputs flagged for a usage-policy violation may be kept up to 2 years; API data is not used for model training. Sources: retention, training (checked 22 Nov 2026).
  • Google LLC (Gemini API) receives the English diary text and voice choice (no name or user ID) to make the natural-voice audio. The app uses Google's paid API: Google does not use the content to improve its products or train AI, and keeps prompts and outputs for 55 days only to detect and prevent abuse. The app sends requests with Google's interaction storage turned off. Sources: usage policies, additional terms, Interactions API (checked 22 Nov 2026).
  • Google Analytics for Firebase receives screen names, feature counts (event names with fixed words such as level, voice or plan), the learning purpose you chose, your plan (free or pass), an app instance ID, device model, OS and language, approximate region derived from IP, and in-app purchase events (product ID and price). Kept for 14 months (Google Analytics data retention setting). Never diary text, words or stories. No advertising ID, IDFV or ad features. On iPhone and iPad, turn it off any time in Settings ("Send to Google Analytics"); the Android app has no such switch yet.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. receives the random app user ID and purchase receipts (and, if you sign in with a pass bought on the web, the web account ID, also random) to check the subscription. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Speech input. On newer iPhones and iPads speech is turned into text on the device. On devices that cannot do this, Apple's speech recognition server is used. On Android, the system speech recognition service (Google) is used. The audio never reaches us.
  • Payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see card details.
  • Content. Stories, audio and photos are downloaded from GitHub Pages and Cloudflare R2; the Android app also downloads the free on-device voice model from jsDelivr and Hugging Face once. These hosts may keep standard connection logs (IP address, time, file).
  • On-device voice (iPhone and iPad). The app downloads the data for making the voice on your device from Hugging Face, once. Your diary is never sent. The download normally happens only on Wi-Fi. Hugging Face may keep standard connection logs (IP address, time, file).
  • App Attest (iPhone and iPad). To confirm that requests come from the genuine app and to prevent abuse, the app sends our relay a key ID made on the device and Apple's proof that the app has not been modified. The relay keeps the key ID and public key; they are not linked to a name or contact details.

Deleting data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. Request counts on the relay expire after 2 days. To delete purchase records held by RevenueCat, email us with the order ID from your App Store or Google Play receipt.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
