Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

English Diary Penguin (formerly Listen English News, "the app") is published by Shohei Yukawa. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

What the app stores on your device

Your diary entries and their audio, saved words, settings (exam, level, language, playback speed, background color, start page) and downloaded stories are stored only on your device (and in your device backup, iCloud on iPhone or Google on Android, if enabled). You can export and import your diary as a file in Settings.

What leaves your device

Diary processing. When you ask the app to tidy your Japanese text, turn it into English, or read it aloud in the natural voice, the text is sent over HTTPS to our relay server (Cloudflare Workers), which passes it to AI services (Anthropic and Google) to produce the result. The relay does not store your diary text. It keeps a daily count of requests per app user ID to apply usage limits.

Speech recognition. When you dictate, your speech is converted to text by your device's speech recognition (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android). The audio is not sent to us.

Content downloads. The app fetches stories, translations and audio from our static content server over HTTPS. Standard server logs (IP address, time, file requested) may be kept briefly by the hosting provider (GitHub Pages).

Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), depending on where you bought them. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts, never your name or email. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Account from the web version. If you bought the pass on our website and choose "Sign in" in Settings, the app receives your account ID (a random identifier, no name or email) so the subscription is recognized. The app does not create accounts.

Usage analytics. We use Google Analytics for Firebase to count which screens are opened and how often features are used. It does not receive your diary text or saved words, and we do not use the advertising identifier.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no sale of data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
